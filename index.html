Our story

Four decades on Center Street.

Total E'Clips has been part of Smithfield for more than forty years. What began as a small salon on Center Street became the place where whole families come, first the grandmother, then her daughters, then the kids who won't sit still.

Four stylists work here now, and each one runs her own chair. That's why the salon has stayed the same size while everything around it changed: nobody is anybody's employee, and nobody is rushing you out to hit a quota.

People still drive up from Logan, Providence and Hyrum for a cut, a color or a perm they could technically get closer to home. They come for the person holding the scissors.

You leave with a haircut you like and, more often than not, the local news.
